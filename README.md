Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview

The Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard is a data analytics project
built in Microsoft Power BI to explore customer demographics,
purchasing patterns, product-category performance, subscription status,
and customer review ratings.

The dashboard turns customer-level data into interactive visual
summaries that can help identify patterns across different age groups
and support data-driven business decisions.

Objectives

Understand the distribution of customers across age groups.

Analyze purchasing behavior and purchase amounts.

Compare purchase activity across product categories.

Review subscription status among customers.

Monitor average customer review ratings.

Explore how customer characteristics relate to purchasing patterns.

Tools and Technologies

Microsoft Power BI Desktop --- data visualization and dashboard
development

Power Query --- data preparation and transformation, where
applicable

DAX --- measures and calculations for dashboard KPIs, where
applicable

Customer behavior dataset --- source data for demographic and
purchasing analysis

Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes KPI cards and visualizations for:

Total Customers --- overall customer count.

Average Purchase Amount --- average spend per purchase record,
based on the measure used in the report.

Average Review Rating --- average rating provided by customers.

Subscription Status --- customer split by subscription status.

Sales by Category --- number of purchase records across product
categories.

Revenue by Category --- total purchase amount by product
category.

Age-Group Analysis --- supports comparison of customer behavior
across demographic groups using the available age and age-group
fields.

The displayed values may change when filters, slicers, or the underlying
data change.
